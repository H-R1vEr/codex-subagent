# Code of Conduct

Participate respectfully, critique claims with evidence and welcome corrections. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, threats and publishing private research or personal information without permission are unacceptable.

Maintainers may clarify expectations, edit/remove violating content, or restrict participation proportionately. Explain enforcement when appropriate and avoid exposing sensitive reports. Scientific disagreement and good-faith uncertainty are welcome.

For nonsensitive concerns, contact maintainers through a repository issue. For private reports, use a maintainer's published private contact when one is configured; do not disclose personal details in public issues. Before public launch, maintainers should configure a private contact route. The template intentionally does not invent an address.
